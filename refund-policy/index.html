Refund Policy

Last Updated: July 7, 2026

Thank you for being a part of the Lucky Cat Mahjong community. Because our in-person events require advance planning, booking venues, and capacity management, we have established the following refund policy to keep things running smoothly for everyone.

1. General Refund Rules

  • Up to 48 Hours Before the Event: We offer full refunds (minus any non-refundable ticketing processing fees charged by Ticket Tailor or Stripe) for cancellations made at least 48 hours before the event’s scheduled start time.
  • Within 48 Hours of the Event: Tickets become non-refundable within 48 hours of the event. At this stage, supplies have been purchased and event details are locked in based on attendance.
  • No-Shows: Refunds or credits will not be issued for missing an event.

2. Ticket Transfers (Pass it to a Friend)

If you can no longer make it but missed the 48-hour refund window, you are always welcome to transfer your ticket to someone else!

  • To transfer a ticket, please contact us via our website or email with the new attendee’s Name and Email Address so we can update our guest list and ensure they receive event details (and we can track their skill level if applicable).

3. Event Cancellations or Rescheduling

We play rain or shine, but life happens. In the rare instance that we have to cancel or reschedule an event due to extreme weather, venue emergencies, or unforeseen circumstances:

  • If Rescheduled: Your ticket will automatically be valid for the new date. If you cannot make the new date, let us know within 7 days of the announcement, and we will issue a full refund.
  • If Cancelled: You will automatically receive a 100% full refund to your original payment method.

4. How to Request a Refund or Transfer

To cancel your ticket and request a refund, click the manage ticket link found directly on our Event Page or within your Confirmation Email to request a self-service refund (available up to 48 hours before the event start time). If you have difficulties, please contact us directly through our website’s contact email, events@luckycatmahjong.com. Please include your Order Number and the Name used to purchase the ticket.

Refunds may take 5–10 business days to reflect on your original payment method.
