Play Mahjong

Play mahjong for free with a coach that helps you get better at playing with points. Best designed to be played on computer.

Game mode

Table rules

  • 3 faan minimum to win. A complete hand under 3 faan can't be declared.
  • Payout rules. On a self-draw every opponent pays the winner. On a discard, only the player who threw the winning tile pays the winner.
  • Limit hand. A hand is capped at 13 faan.
  • Dealer. The dealer keeps dealing after winning. Otherwise it moves on, and the round wind changes after everyone has dealt.
