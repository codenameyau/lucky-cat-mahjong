Table rules

  • 3 faan minimum to win. A complete hand under 3 faan can't be declared.
  • On a self-draw every opponent pays the winner. On a discard, only the player who threw the winning tile pays.
  • Flowers and seasons are set aside and replaced. Your seat's flower or season is worth 1 faan, and having none is worth 1 faan.
  • Dealer. The dealer keeps dealing after winning. Otherwise it moves on, and the round wind changes after everyone has dealt.
