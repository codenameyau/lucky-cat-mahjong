Table rules

  • 3 faan to win. A complete hand under 3 faan can't be declared.
  • Who pays. On a self-draw every opponent pays the winner. On a discard, only the player who threw the winning tile pays.
  • Claims. Win beats pung and kong, which beat chow. You can only chow from the player on your left.
  • Flowers and seasons are set aside and replaced. Your seat's flower or season is worth 1 faan, and having none is worth 1 faan.
  • Dealer. The dealer keeps the deal after winning or a drawn hand. Otherwise it moves on, and the round wind changes after everyone has dealt.

Tap a tile to pick it, tap again to discard. On a keyboard: arrow keys pick, Enter discards, Esc opens the menu.
