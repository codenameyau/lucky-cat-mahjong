Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

Your privacy is incredibly important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Lucky Cat Mahjong collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit luckycatmahjong.com, purchase tickets to our events, or interact with our community.

In short

  • We collect your name, email and Instagram handle when you give them to us, and use them to run our events and tell you about upcoming ones.
  • We use Google Analytics to understand our visitors: where to host mahjong events, and how mahjong's trends and audience are growing.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we don't share it with marketers or other businesses. The one exception is Google signals in Google Analytics, explained in section 4.
  • You can turn analytics off at any time in Your Privacy Choices, or with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide to us, information needed to fulfill your ticket orders, and information about how our website is used.

  • Ticketing and Account Information: when you buy a ticket, we collect your name and email address.
  • Contact and Social Information: your name, email address and Instagram handle when you share them with us. For example, you might finish our tile personality quiz, ask about a lesson or booking (the form on our homepage opens an email to us), email us, or message us on Instagram.
  • Supplementary Event Questions: your answers to event-specific questions asked during checkout or in the quiz, such as your mahjong skill level, to help us organize and optimize the event experience.
  • Location Information: the city, neighborhood or zip code you live in, to help us understand where our community is and plan future events in convenient locations.
  • Media (Photos & Videos): we capture photography and video footage at our live events. This media may be used on our website, social media channels, and future promotional materials to showcase our community.
  • Website Usage Information: when you visit our website, Google Analytics records the pages you view and the buttons you click, your browser and device, the site that sent you, and your approximate location worked out from your IP address. See section 4.
  • Payment Data: payment processing is handled securely by our third-party billing partners via our ticketing platform, Ticket Tailor. We do not store or have direct access to your credit card details.
  • What We Do NOT Collect: we do not collect or store your full physical address.

Our mahjong game and quiz keep your settings, saved games and quiz progress in your browser's local storage. That stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to run our events, support our community, and understand our visitors. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Process your ticket purchases and send you order confirmations.
  • Send you critical updates about events you are attending (for example, time changes and event logistics).
  • Use your skill level, location, and other preferences to structure event activities, seating, matchups, and geographic event planning.
  • Share news, upcoming events, and special announcements through our own mailing list and Instagram. This is targeted marketing of our own events. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Promote our community and events using the photos and videos captured during our gatherings.
  • Understand how our website is used, decide where to host mahjong events, and follow mahjong's trends and demographics, using Google Analytics.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with marketers or other businesses. We never sell the names, emails and Instagram handles you give us to advertisers.

We use a few service providers that handle information for us, only to run our website and events:

  • Ticketing Platform: Ticket Tailor securely manages event registration, ticket fulfillment and payments.
  • Website Analytics & Hosting: Google Analytics measures how our website is used (see section 4), Google Forms receives the quiz form, and GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Partners and Collaborators: we work with venues and businesses to host and promote shared events. We don't give them your contact information. Where a partner sells tickets or runs sign-ups itself, what you give them is covered by their own privacy policy.
  • Legal Requirements: we will disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect the safety and anonymity of our community and its members.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics is our analytics service provider. From this website it receives the pages you view and the buttons you click, a random visitor ID and visit ID (never your name, email or Instagram handle), your approximate location, and device information. In our Google Analytics account we have turned on:

  • Google signals: for visitors who are signed in to Google and allow ad personalization, Google may link this website's analytics data to their Google account. That lets us see reports across devices and demographics (such as age range, gender and interests) as totals across many visitors. It also supports Google's advertising features. Under California law (CCPA/CPRA) this may count as “sharing” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. See California residents to opt out.
  • User-ID: our random visitor ID, so your visits and sessions are counted as one visitor.
  • User-provided data collection: this lets a website send Google details its visitors have given it. Today our website does not send your name, email or Instagram handle to Google Analytics.
  • Granular location and device data: city-level location and more detailed device information, such as the model and browser version.

We have also accepted Google's User Data Collection Acknowledgement, Google's terms for collecting this data. Google's own use of it is described in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can block or clear cookies in your browser's settings. Blocking all cookies may cause the embedded ticket checkout to malfunction.

5. Data Security

We take data security seriously and use industry-standard physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your personal information. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You are always in control of your data. You have the right to:

  • Ask us what personal data we hold about you.
  • Request that we correct any incorrect or outdated information.
  • Request that we delete your personal data from our active marketing systems.
  • Unsubscribe from our mailing list at any time.

To make a request, email events@luckycatmahjong.com.

Analytics

Analytics cookies are on in this browser.

  • Turning analytics off deletes our lcm_vid and lcm_sid cookies and Google's _ga cookies, and our website stops sending anything to Google Analytics from this browser. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, analytics stays off until you choose to turn it on.
  • Global Privacy Control: if your browser sends this signal, our website sends nothing to Google Analytics from it, unless you turn analytics on here.
  • Google's tools: Google Ads Settings turns off ad personalization, which also keeps you out of Google signals. Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on stops Google Analytics on every website.
  • Your browser: you can block or clear cookies at any time.

California residents

California law gives you the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of your personal information. We don't sell personal information. The only sharing as California defines it may be Google signals (section 4). To opt out of that sharing:

  • use Turn analytics off above, or Cookie settings at the bottom of any page;
  • turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser, which our website treats as an opt-out; or
  • email events@luckycatmahjong.com with “Do Not Share My Personal Information”.

7. How Long We Keep It

Cookies last as long as the table in section 4 says. Google Analytics keeps visit data for the retention period set in our Google Analytics account, then deletes it. We keep your contact information until you ask us to remove it.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our events, our website or legal requirements. When we do, we will update the “Effective Date” at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, email events@luckycatmahjong.com or message @luckycat.mahjong on Instagram.
