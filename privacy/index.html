Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

This page explains what Lucky Cat Mahjong collects on luckycatmahjong.com, why we collect it, the cookies the site sets, and how to opt out or reach us.

In short

  • We use Google Analytics to see how people use the site.
  • We use what we learn only to understand our visitors better: where to host mahjong events, and how mahjong's trends and audience are growing.
  • When you give us your name, email or Instagram handle, we use it to tell you about our events.
  • We don't sell your information to advertisers, and we never sell the details you give us to advertisers.
  • You can turn analytics cookies off at any time, below or with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

What we collect

What you give us

We collect names, email addresses and Instagram handles when you share them with us, for example when you:

  • finish the tile personality quiz (your name, Instagram handle, zip code and mahjong skill level, so we can invite you to games near you);
  • ask about a lesson or booking (the form on our homepage opens an email to us with what you typed);
  • email us, message us on Instagram, or buy a ticket to one of our events.

What Google Analytics collects

When you visit, Google Analytics records the pages you view and the buttons you click (for example, booking buttons, quiz steps, and the buttons in our mahjong game), along with your browser and device, the site that sent you, and your approximate location worked out from your IP address. In our Google Analytics account we have turned on:

  • Google signals: for visitors signed in to Google who allow ad personalization, Google adds information such as age range, gender and interests, and links visits across devices. We see this only as totals across many visitors.
  • User-ID: our own random visitor ID (see Cookies), so your visits and sessions are counted as one visitor. It is a random code, never your name or email.
  • User-provided data collection: this lets a site send Google details its visitors have given it. Today the website does not send your name, email or Instagram handle to Google Analytics.
  • Granular location and device data: city-level location and more detailed device information, such as the model and browser version.

We have also accepted Google's User Data Collection Acknowledgement, Google's terms for collecting this data. Google's own use of it is covered by how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

What stays in your browser

The mahjong game and the quiz keep your settings, saved games and quiz progress in your browser's local storage. That stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

How we use it

  • Analytics: to understand our visitors better: where to host mahjong events, which parts of the site people use, and how mahjong's trends and audience (demographics) are growing.
  • Your name, email and Instagram handle: for targeted marketing of our events, such as invitations and news about games, lessons and events we think you'll like, and to answer you when you write to us.

We don't sell your information to advertisers. We never sell the names, emails and Instagram handles you give us to advertisers.

Who else handles it

We use a few services to run the site and our events, and they handle your information for us:

  • Google: Google Analytics, and Google Forms, which receives the quiz form.
  • Ticket Tailor: sells tickets on our Events page, under its own privacy policy.
  • Instagram (Meta): when you message us there.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.

Your choices and opting out

Analytics cookies are on in this browser.

  • Analytics cookies: turning analytics off deletes our lcm_vid and lcm_sid cookies and Google's _ga cookies, and the site stops sending anything to Google Analytics from this browser. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, analytics stays off until you choose to turn it on.
  • Global Privacy Control: if your browser sends this signal, analytics stays off unless you turn it on here.
  • Google: Google's Analytics opt-out add-on stops Google Analytics on every site, and My Ad Center turns off ad personalization, which also keeps you out of Google signals.
  • Event marketing: to stop hearing from us, or to ask what we have about you, correct it or delete it, email us at events@luckycatmahjong.com or message @luckycat.mahjong on Instagram.

How long we keep it

Cookies last as long as the table above says. Google Analytics keeps visit data for the retention period set in our Google Analytics account, then deletes it. We keep your name, email and Instagram handle until you ask us to remove them.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions? Email events@luckycatmahjong.com.
