# Only Orphans: training progress

The league rounds (1 to 5) were generated by `npm run ml:orphans` (`scripts/ml/orphans.js`). From O5, training runs in the lab (`npm run ml:lab -- --strategy only-orphans`), and its rounds are below. How the model is trained and what it plays is in [the playbook](only-orphans.md).

## Where it stands

- **Games played:** 350,000: 250,000 in 5 league rounds and 100,000 in 2 lab rounds.
- **Champion:** `OL2c` (lab round 2, from O5), +26.8 ± 14.8 points a hand against the rule-based baseline on 20,000 deals.
- **In the game:** `OL2c` (`js/mahjong-only-orphans-weights.js`).

## Lab rounds

Each candidate takes one seat at the game's table (Neural Cat, Jorge Yau, Lucky Cat), on the same 6,000 deals as the champion. The best two then play 20,000 fresh deals. J is points a hand + 5 × hands won (in percentage points) against the champion, and a candidate is kept only if it's ahead beyond its standard error. A kept champion ships to the game. Round 1 played NC1 and LC20. From round 2 the table is NC2 and lc23.

## League rounds

Each model takes one seat against Neural Cat, Jorge Yau and Lucky Cat, on the same deals as the best model, the model in the game and the rule-based baseline (9,000 deals, each played from every seat, new deals every round; rounds 1 to 4 used 3,000). Scores are points a hand, with the 95% margin. "Kept" means it beat the best and became the best. A milestone is a best that also beats the baseline by more than the margin and scores more than the model in the game on the same deals: it ships to the game.

The opponents are whatever the game seats: from round 4, Neural Cat NC1, Lucky Cat's network LC20 and the newer Jorge Yau (rounds 1 to 3 played the models and bots before them), so scores from different tables don't compare directly.

## The rule-based baseline

On lab round 2's 20,000 deals, it scored +22.3 points a hand, winning 3.3% of hands and dealing in on 4.6%. On league round 5's ranking deals, the rule-based Only Orphans scored +8.0 points a hand ± 14, winning 3.4% of hands (270 limit hands, 261 Thirteen Orphans) and dealing in on 4.1%, with an average win of 2,747 points.
