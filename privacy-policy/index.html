Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

We do not sell your personal information. This policy explains what we collect when you visit our website, buy tickets to our events or join our community, how we use it, and the choices you have.

In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Lucky Cat Mahjong, and “our website” means luckycatmahjong.com and its pages, including the mahjong game and the tile quiz.

1. Information We Collect

We collect what you give us directly, and some information automatically when you use our website.

Information you give us

  • Ticketing and account information: your name and email address when you buy a ticket.
  • Event questions: your answers to questions asked at checkout, such as your mahjong skill level.
  • Location: the city and neighborhood you live in, which you may share with us.
  • Social media: your Instagram handle, if you share it with us.
  • Payment data: handled by our ticketing platform, Ticket Tailor, and its payment partners. We never see or store your card details.
  • Photos and videos: we take photos and video at our live events, and you may appear in them.

Information collected automatically on our website

  • Pages and clicks: the pages you view, the buttons you click, how far you get in the tile quiz, and how the mahjong game is played.
  • A visitor ID: a random ID stored in a cookie, which lets us recognize the same browser across visits and sessions. It is never your name or email.
  • Device and approximate location: your device type, browser, operating system and screen size, and your approximate location (such as city or zip code), as estimated by Google Analytics from your IP address.

What we do not collect: your full physical address or your card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to run our events, grow our community and improve our website:

  • Process ticket purchases and send order confirmations.
  • Send updates about events you are attending, such as time changes and logistics.
  • Plan seating, matchups and activities using your skill level and preferences.
  • Decide where to host future events, and understand mahjong trends and our community's demographics, using location and analytics data.
  • Send news, upcoming events and announcements from our own mailing list, including event marketing tailored to your city, interests and skill level. You can unsubscribe from any email.
  • Understand how our website is used and improve it.
  • Promote our community with photos and videos from our events.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers, marketers or other businesses for their own use.

We give it only to service providers that work on our behalf, and only what they need:

  • Ticket Tailor, our ticketing platform, to handle registration, tickets and payments.
  • Google Analytics, to measure how our website is used (see below).
  • Our website host and email provider, to run the website and send our emails.

We will also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our community and its members.

Google Analytics and Google signals

Our website uses Google Analytics with Google signals, User-ID, and granular location and device data turned on. Google receives the pages you view and the buttons you click, our random visitor ID, your approximate location and your device details. With Google signals, Google may link this data to the Google accounts of signed-in users who have turned on ad personalization. This lets us see reports across devices, and lets Google use the data in its advertising features. Under some laws, including California's, this may count as “sharing” personal information for advertising. You can opt out, as described in sections 4 and 6. How Google uses this data is set out in Google's policy on partner sites.

Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, our website does not set the visitor ID or session marker and does not send your activity to Google Analytics. We treat it as a request to opt out of sharing.

Your choices. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them may stop the embedded ticket checkout from working. You can also install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, and turn off ad personalization in Google's My Ad Center.

5. Data Security and Retention

We use industry-standard physical, electronic and procedural safeguards to protect your information. No method of sending or storing data over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

We keep your information only as long as we need it:

  • Ticket and contact details: kept indefinitely, until you ask us to delete them.
  • Website analytics: Google Analytics keeps visit-level data for 14 months, the longest it allows, and keeps its summary reports indefinitely.
  • Photos and videos: as long as they are used to promote our community.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You are in control of your data. You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Stop sending you marketing email. Each email also has an unsubscribe link.
  • Stop sharing your website activity with Google for advertising (see section 7).

To opt out of website analytics, use the choices in section 4. We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights.

7. California Residents: Your Privacy Choices

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) gives you these rights over your personal information:

  • Know what we collect, use and disclose, and get a copy of it.
  • Correct inaccurate information and delete it.
  • Opt out of sharing. We do not sell personal information. Our use of Google signals may count as “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising. To opt out, turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser, which our website honors automatically, or email us (section 9).
  • Not be discriminated against for using these rights.

We will confirm your identity before acting on a request, and answer within 45 days. An authorized agent may make a request for you.

8. Children

Our website and events are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Miscellaneous

Changes to this policy. We may update this policy as our events, website or the law change. When we do, we will update the Effective Date at the top. For a significant change, we will also tell our mailing list.

Contact us. For questions, or to use any of your rights, email us at events@luckycatmahjong.com or reach us through our website's contact channels.
